## Part 3: Critical Synthesis & Key Insights

1. **The Human-AI Dynamic is Adversarial yet Generative:**
   The human operator acts as a high-velocity conductor operating from mobile hardware in the physical world. The AI models (Claude, Gemini, Mistral, Grok) act as overconfident, fluent, yet error-prone interlocutors.
2. **The "Comment Layer Blindness" Paradox:**
   Standard LLM evaluation tools inspect only repo titles, READMEs, and issue descriptions—missing 80%+ of real work buried in issue comments and binary drops. OEF exposes this structural blind spot.
3. **The Proof is in the Permanent Receipt:**
   By binding every mistake, hallucination, and mishearing to Git commits and Zenodo DOIs, the repository transforms fleeting model hallucinations into permanent, citation-ready research data.
